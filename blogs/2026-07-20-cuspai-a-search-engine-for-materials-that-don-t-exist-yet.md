The next breakthroughs in semiconductors, batteries, carbon capture, and clean water depend on materials that have yet to be discovered. CuspAI is building an AI-powered search engine for materials. Its platform works backward from the properties a material needs, generating and evaluating new candidates based on both performance and whether they can actually be made.
